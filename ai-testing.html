FINDING · SKILL FILE · cash-flow-snapshot HIGH

A mid-October cash forecast can miss the BAS due on 28 October

The skill builds the forecast from balances, money owed both ways and fixed costs. Tax and super aren't in its instructions.

EXPECTED
BAS, PAYG and super show as outflows on their due dates
ACTUAL
Not instructed. Sometimes covered by the model, not guaranteed
Fix line: “GST registered, quarterly BAS. Add BAS, PAYG and super due dates as outflows in every forecast.”
